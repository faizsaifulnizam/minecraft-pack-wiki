## Coverage still being researched

Tensura/Solo progression, four independent magic curricula, dimensions and bosses, equipment, food/building and multiplayer need deeper mechanic-specific guides. Installed presence does not mean those guides or integrations are complete. Use the pack map to identify a system; missing instructions are not proof a feature is absent.

Search matches literal terms, aliases and exact identifiers. Try a short mod/item name rather than expecting semantic answers to a long question. Full-spoiler search is a separate explicit option. Ordinary topic links are spoiler-safe entry points. Optional full-spoiler documents are available through clearly labelled reveal links; full-spoiler search remains opt-in.
