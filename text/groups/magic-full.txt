# Make your first Ars Nouveau utility spell

## Baseline

Candidate **dev-0.5-20261015-r2 (unapplied)**. The versioned description below is imported from previous-baseline retained witnesses for dev-0.5-20261015; it is not a fresh candidate archive revalidation. Effective recipes, settings and gameplay remain unverified.

**dev-0.5-20261015** — Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.250; Ars Nouveau **5.13.3**.

## Evidence status

Bounded retained-evidence static review only; candidate dev-0.5-20261015-r2 remains unapplied. This is not runtime validation, integration approval, baseline adoption or publication.

Tutorial based on hash-matched shipped recipes and English documentation. Not gameplay tested. Ingredient tags and recipes describe shipped intent, not resolved pack overrides. Ars spells are assembled from glyphs; this is not the scroll-inscription route used by [Iron's Spellbooks](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/guides/iron-first-scroll/).

## Prerequisites

Set aside a book, an iron shovel, iron pickaxe, iron axe and iron sword for the Novice Spell Book. These are crafting ingredients, so do not use your only working tools. For a Scribe's Table, collect three Archwood Slabs, two gold nuggets and two Archwood Logs. Keep experience available. The two optional glyph recipes below each declare **`exp: 27`**; the experience-unit implementation was not independently traced. Check the displayed cost rather than reading that as 27 levels. [A01]

Work in a clear area away from containers, animals and valuable blocks. Use a utility spell before experimenting with destructive effects. This is safety advice, not a tested protection mechanism.

## Steps

1. **Craft the Novice Spell Book.** Combine the ordinary book and the four iron tools in a shapeless recipe. You should receive `ars_nouveau:novice_spell_book`. A separate documentation item, `ars_nouveau:worn_notebook`, is displayed as **Tattered Tome** in this version; make it from a book and a lapis-tag gem if you want the shipped documentation. The alternate novice-book recipe consumes that tome instead of an ordinary book, so keep a separate copy if you want both. [A01]
2. **Build a Scribe's Table.** In the crafting grid put three Archwood Slabs across the top, gold nuggets at middle left and right, and Archwood Logs at bottom left and right. Place the table somewhere you can reach. Use your spell book on it to open the glyph codex. [A02]
3. **Check for Touch and Conjure Magelight.** If either is missing from your learned glyphs, select it in the table's codex and choose **Select**. Supply the displayed ingredients by throwing them onto the table: Touch takes one button accepted by the `minecraft:buttons` tag; Conjure Magelight takes one lantern and one torch. Supply the recipe's experience cost as well. The table can also pull from nearby inventories, so keep unrelated materials away while learning. [A03]
4. **Learn each produced glyph.** Consume the crafted glyph item. Crafting it alone does not add it to your spellcrafting list. If the glyph is already learned, do not craft another just for this tutorial. [A03]
5. **Open spellcrafting with the spell book selected.** Use your configured spellcrafting control. The shipped text prints **[C]**, but this is not a verified saved keybinding. Choose a free bookmark, add **Touch**, then **Conjure Magelight**, give the spell a name, and choose **Create**. Clicking an unwanted glyph in the assembled spell removes it. A spell must start with exactly one Form; Touch is the targeting Form here. [A04]
6. **Try the spell against an expendable solid block.** Use the book's cast action while aiming at that block. The Light description says a block-targeted cast creates a permanent light source. Your mana should fall when the spell casts and regenerate over time. The cast binding and the appearance of the result still need a runtime check; no key is assumed here. [A05]

## Expected result

You have a Novice Spell Book, learned utility glyphs and a saved two-glyph spell. The documented checkpoint is a light at the target and mana expenditure, not a combat victory. Continue with [Source production and the Ars workshop](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/reference/ars-workshop/) only after you can save and select this spell.

## Common failures

- **The glyph was crafted but is absent from the list:** consume the glyph item, then reopen spellcrafting. [A03]
- **The spell cannot be constructed:** put one Form first; remove extra Forms and unsupported augments. The Novice book casts tier-I glyphs only. Do not spend materials on higher-tier glyphs for this book. [A04]
- **The table seems to take items unexpectedly:** its shipped documentation says it pulls nearby inventories. The Dominion Wand can disable that behavior, but crafting the wand is outside this starter route. Move unrelated stock away meanwhile. [A03]
- **Casting stops after repeated attempts:** wait for mana regeneration before changing your Source setup. Mana is the casting resource; Source powers workshop processes. Their co-presence does not make them interchangeable. [A05, A06]
- **The controls do not match the text:** inspect the game's current Controls screen rather than changing keys based on this manuscript. No current binding was verified.

# Put a first scroll into an Iron's spell book

## Baseline

**dev-0.5-20261015-r2 (candidate; unapplied)** — Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.250; baseline lists Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks **1.21.1-3.16.3**.

## Evidence status

Bounded retained-evidence static review only; candidate dev-0.5-20261015-r2 remains unapplied. This is not runtime validation, integration approval, baseline adoption or publication.

**Candidate-specific static evidence; not runtime tested.** These revisions target **dev-0.5-20261015-r2**, an unapplied candidate, not the accepted dev-0.5-20261015 baseline. Retained exact text witnesses belong to `irons_spellbooks-1.21.1-3.16.3.jar`, SHA-256 `d01937925160e32bb68910262af861d168e1d792808a29dfb596dc94bdff5c75`. That differs from the old baseline hash; the mismatch establishes different bytes, not their cause, archive origin or changed gameplay. Shipped-book statements are labelled as documentation, not demonstrated behavior; unversioned-only details remain provisional. Effective overrides, controls and gameplay have not been tested. [I00]

## Prerequisites

Have a safely obtained scroll and a spell book, found or crafted. No particular structure, enemy or drop chance is required by this route. If you do not yet have a scroll, the [workstation reference](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/reference/iron-workstations/) explains the documented crafting alternative; it does not promise that the ingredients are available in your world.

To craft the candidate Ironbound Tome (Iron Spell Book), reserve **three chains, four leather-tag items and two paper**. For an Inscription Table, reserve **one book, three wooden slabs and two wooden fences**. You need an ordinary crafting grid as well. [I01, I02]


**Identify your book before committing a scroll.** The retained translation calls `irons_spellbooks:iron_spell_book` **Ironbound Tome** and the copper book **Flimsy Journal**. The shipped Early Game entry points to the copper recipe as a beginner option; the Ironbound Tome below remains a valid independent construction route, not a required first tier. An optional Iron’s Guidebook recipe combines one ordinary book and one Arcane Essence, but explicitly requires Patchouli to be loaded and produces `patchouli:guide_book` with book component `irons_spellbooks:iss_guide_book`. A documentation book is not the casting book. No guidebook craft or Patchouli loading was tested. [I08, I09]

## Steps

1. **Craft the Ironbound Tome (Iron Spell Book) if you need a book.** Put chains down the left column, leather across the two remaining cells of the top and bottom rows, and paper in the two remaining middle-row cells. The retained candidate recipe produces `irons_spellbooks:iron_spell_book`. Keep this system separate from Ars spellcrafting; this object produces the Iron item, not an Ars book. [I01]
2. **Craft an Inscription Table.** Put a book in the top-right cell, wooden slabs across the middle row and wooden fences in the bottom-left and bottom-right cells. Place the resulting table at your base, not inside an active fight. [I02]
3. **Decide whether to save the scroll.** The documentation distinguishes two uses: consuming a scroll casts it once without mana or cooldown, while inscription puts its spell in a book. If you want repeat use, take it to the table instead of consuming it. The retained Early Game entry says retrieving a scroll uninscribes its spell. This documents an intended recovery route for editable books, not a tested guarantee. Unique books are a documented exception: their existing spells cannot be removed or edited. [I08] [I03]
4. **Open the table and place your book and scroll in its interface.** The guide describes slotting the scroll's spell into the book here. Check whether the book accepts that scroll before taking a result. Rarity can make a scroll too powerful for a particular book; this is a book/scroll constraint, not evidence that another magic mod must be progressed first. [I03]
5. **Inspect the resulting book's spell list.** Confirm that the intended spell is listed. This is the first checkpoint; do not assume a crafted empty book already contains spells. The retained English interface text includes “This scroll is too rare for this spell book.” This supports the rarity warning, but not numeric slot counts or ceilings. [I08] [I03]
6. **Prepare a separate casting check.** Find the mod's current casting/selection controls in the game's Controls screen, read the chosen spell's target and cost information, and use an expendable target in an open practice area. No binding, friendly-fire rule or spell-specific safe range has been verified here. If you cannot establish a safe target, stop at inscription rather than testing on a pet or player.

**Checkpoint and recovery:** note the scroll’s spell/level and the book’s existing spell list before inscription. Inspect the intended result before taking it; if it differs, stop rather than consuming a rare scroll or overwriting a valued slot. Afterward compare the actual spell list with that note. The shipped book documents scroll retrieval for editable books, but this guide does not invent a removal button or promise item recovery after an unexpected result. [I08]

## Expected result

The shipped-book-documented outcome is a book containing the chosen scroll's spell, ready for a later controlled casting check. It is not proof of a working local recipe, active mana settings or compatibility with another mod's power system. Once inscription is understood, use [ink, rarity and workstation checks](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/reference/iron-workstations/) to plan the next spell without wasting a rare scroll.

## Common failures

- **The book rejects a scroll:** compare the scroll's rarity with the book's supported rarity before making another table. The documentation says a better book may be needed. Exact local caps remain unresolved. [I03]
- **You cast the scroll instead of saving it:** the guide explicitly describes consumption as a separate one-use path. Do not consume your only copy when your goal is inscription. No undo is promised. [I03]
- **The crafting grid gives a different result or nothing:** record the recipe ID, displayed inputs and result as a discrepancy; static archive evidence does not establish effective recipe precedence. Do not rename, replace or reinstall the JAR on this guide's authority. [I00]
- **A saved spell does not cast:** binding, mana, cooldown and spell conditions need a runtime check. The no-mana/no-cooldown statement applies to consumed scrolls, not an unlimited spell-book mode. [I03]
- **You encounter hostile magic while gathering:** withdraw to ordinary shelter and retain your normal equipment. This tutorial does not require fighting an identified boss or raiding a specific structure.

# Start a Nature's Aura garden and measure its Aura

## Baseline

Candidate **dev-0.5-20261015-r2 (unapplied)**. The versioned description below is imported from previous-baseline retained witnesses for dev-0.5-20261015; it is not a fresh candidate archive revalidation. Effective recipes, settings and gameplay remain unverified.

**dev-0.5-20261015** — Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.250; Nature's Aura **41.10**.

## Evidence status

Bounded retained-evidence static review only; candidate dev-0.5-20261015-r2 remains unapplied. This is not runtime validation, integration approval, baseline adoption or publication.

Tutorial from hash-matched recipes and shipped Book of Natural Aura entries. Not gameplay tested. Patchouli renders that book; it is not an independent magic system. The book's ritual structure is identified, but its exact code-defined geometry has not been independently transcribed here. Use its in-game structure page rather than a guessed ring diagram. [N00]

## Prerequisites

Bring two paper, one leather and any accepted sapling for the book. For one batch of Brilliant Fiber, collect four leaf blocks, four gold nuggets and one Short Grass item. Keep access to a grown tree and an oak sapling with enough space to grow. For an Environmental Eye ritual, reserve one spider eye, one gold ingot and two Gold Leaf; those are ingredient items in addition to the central oak sapling and the setup materials. [N01, N02, N04]

Work away from your main garden while learning Aura consumption. The shipped guide warns that draining an area and dimension changes affect behavior. If conditions worsen, stop adding consumers and leave the affected area; no instant restoration or universal safe radius is established. [N05]

## Steps

1. **Craft the Book of Natural Aura.** Combine two paper, leather and a sapling, shapeless. The output is a Patchouli guide book carrying `patchouli:book = naturesaura:book`; a generic empty book is not the same item. Its orange icons point toward next research steps, while red icons mark newly unlocked chapters. [N00, N01]
2. **Craft four Brilliant Fiber.** Arrange leaves at the four corners, gold nuggets at the four edge centers and Short Grass in the center. Do not substitute wheat seeds or an ordinary Grass Block. The result is `naturesaura:gold_fiber`, count four. [N02]
3. **Apply Brilliant Fiber to a tree's crown and wait.** The Brilliant Trees entry describes this as the way to produce a tree that sheds Gold Leaf when harvested. Watch for the transformed foliage before harvesting. Conversion timing, tree-size limits and the exact interaction gesture are not established by the retained text; follow the book's presentation and do not assume an immediate result. [N02]
4. **Divide your Gold Leaf between setup and products.** One leaf crafts into two Gold Powder. One leaf above one log crafts one Wooden Stand. Do not turn every leaf into powder: the Eye recipe itself consumes two leaf. [N03, N04]
5. **Prepare the Ritual of the Forest.** Open that book entry and reproduce its Gold Powder configuration around the central sapling position. Put the recipe ingredients on Wooden Stands around the setup. The text says not every shown stand is required, but it does not specify a stand radius; do not infer one from another magic mod. This manuscript cannot replace the missing geometry with a prose approximation. [N03]
6. **Make an Environmental Eye.** Use an **oak** sapling in the center and provide one spider eye, one gold ingot and two Gold Leaf on stands. Grow the central sapling, naturally or using fertilizer, to start the ritual. The ritual consumes the growing tree while transforming the ingredients. Check for `naturesaura:eye` as the recipe result. [N03, N04]
7. **Move the Eye into your hotbar.** It does not need to be actively selected according to the shipped text. Check the top-left Aura bar: half full represents the expected default level, not an empty environment. The Eye can also show stored Aura for Aura-bearing blocks. [N04]

## Expected result

You have the book, a Gold Leaf supply and a way to observe Aura before committing to consuming devices. The Eye is the handoff to [the Natural Altar and sustainable Aura reference](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/reference/natures-aura-workshop/). Do not use a full-looking bar as proof of unlimited capacity; the basic Eye cannot quantify beyond the ends of its displayed scale. [N04]

## Common failures

- **A chapter is missing:** the shipped book ties chapter visibility to advancements and highlights next steps. Its `tree_ritual` entry declares `naturesaura:gold_leaf`; the Eye entry declares `naturesaura:wood_stand`. These are book conditions, not proof of a universal code-enforced crafting lock. [N00]
- **A sapling grows but no ritual result appears:** compare the powder arrangement, stand ingredients and exact sapling species with the chosen recipe. Different rituals require different saplings. Exact geometry remains an explicit documentation gap. [N03]
- **You cannot make the setup after crafting powder:** retain leaf for stands and recipe ingredients as well. [N03, N04]
- **Aura seems absent at half a bar:** that is the documented normal level. Consult the [measurement and depletion checks](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/reference/natures-aura-workshop/), not Ars Source Jars; no cross-system power conversion is established. [N04, N05]

# Prepare your first Occultism work area

## Baseline

Candidate **dev-0.5-20261015-r2 (unapplied)**. The versioned description below is imported from previous-baseline retained witnesses for dev-0.5-20261015; it is not a fresh candidate archive revalidation. Effective recipes, settings and gameplay remain unverified.

**dev-0.5-20261015** — Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.250; Occultism **1.224.5**.

## Evidence status

Bounded retained-evidence static review only; candidate dev-0.5-20261015-r2 remains unapplied. This is not runtime validation, integration approval, baseline adoption or publication.

Beginner tutorial from hash-matched shipped recipes, Dictionary of Spirits structures and their English translation entries. Not runtime tested. Modonomicon supplies this book's presentation; it is not a fifth magic progression system. This safe route reaches a usable conversion area and ritual preparation. Optional discovery and the first summoning are separated onto a full-spoiler page.

## Prerequisites

Start with ordinary shelter and a small farm. Bring a book, spare andesite and access to flint and steel. Reserve one feather, one black-dye-tag item and an additional ordinary book for later conversions. Do not consume unfamiliar farm produce while exploring: the shipped guide describes potentially negative side effects. Wait somewhere secure if affected, and postpone combat or hazardous travel. Exact duration and recovery items have not been verified. [O01, O02]

## Steps

1. **Get the book's seed ingredient.** Break grass to look for Demon's Dream seeds, as the shipped guide describes. Keep one seed for crafting and grow additional plants for fruit; no drop probability or growth time is established here. Craft a Dictionary of Spirits from one accepted datura-seed-tag item and one book-tag item, shapeless. [O01]
2. **Prepare a separate conversion spot.** The guide says to throw a grown `occultism:datura` fruit onto the ground and light it with flint and steel to make Spirit Fire. Clear ordinary flammables from your ignition area first. The resulting Spirit Fire is documented as not harming living beings, but that does not establish that the initial ignition or surrounding ordinary fire is harmless. Do not try this beside your storage. [O02]
3. **Convert one andesite as a test.** Drop it into the Spirit Fire. The recipe produces one `occultism:otherstone`. Check that exact item ID/name before committing a stack. This avoids the need to pursue the book's optional discovery route just to obtain a basic crafting material. [O03]
4. **Make five sacrificial bowls.** Each ordinary bowl uses five Otherstone in a shallow U: `o o` above `ooo`. Upgrade one bowl to a Golden Sacrificial Bowl by surrounding it with eight gold ingots. You now have one central golden bowl and four ordinary ingredient bowls. [O04]
5. **Prepare binding materials without starting a ritual.** Convert the spare black dye into Purified Ink, feather into Awakened Feather and book into a Taboo Book, one item per conversion. The book's basic binding recipe also uses four blue dyes. Keep these materials labelled and separate from ordinary ink, feathers and books. [O05]
6. **Prepare basic chalk.** The recipe uses three chalk-base dust ingredients and three Otherworld-wood dust ingredients in two vertical columns. In this archive, the retained dust tags name **Burnt Otherstone** and **Otherworld Ashes**, respectively. Smelt Otherstone into Polished Otherstone, then smelt it again into Burnt Otherstone. Otherworld Ashes come from converting an accepted Otherworld log in Spirit Fire; the [Full-spoiler content omitted] explains the tree/harvest route and its discovery mechanics. Craft Impure White Chalk from three of each material, then convert it in Spirit Fire to White Chalk. [O06]
7. **Choose your next checkpoint.** If you only wanted basic conversion, you can already produce Otherstone and the three binding ingredients. For a prepared first summoning, finish the chalk materials and then consult [Full-spoiler content omitted]. That page names the summon, layout, ingredients and expected result. Do not start an unfamiliar ritual merely to see what appears.

## Expected result

You have the Dictionary, a one-item conversion checkpoint, a central activation bowl, four ingredient bowls and the binding-material chain. The chalk recipe is traced through its shipped tags rather than inferred from names; its log-harvesting route and the complete first summoning are in the full procedure. This safe page deliberately stops before those discoveries, not at an unexplained material gap.

For a returning reader who already has chalk, the [ritual troubleshooting reference](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/reference/occultism-workbench/) explains bowl placement, waiting states and book naming without revealing later entities or rewards.

## Common failures

- **The result is the wrong stone:** use andesite for the retained Spirit Fire recipe. Otherrock is documented as useful for decoration and bowls, but not a substitute for all chalk/storage bases. [O03]
- **A binding book is rejected later:** an unbound book is not the activation item. Bind the correct book type with the Dictionary as described on the full procedure page. [O05]

## Evidence status

Reference and workshop how-to from hash-matched shipped English documentation and recipes. No device, automation interface or save/reopen behavior was exercised. Start with [a saved utility spell](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/guides/ars-first-spell/); this page does not add another mod's power system as a prerequisite.

## Prerequisites

Keep amethyst shards or lapis, Archwood Planks, gold and access to a crop/tree-growing area. A basic Imbuement Chamber uses six Archwood Planks and two gold ingots. A Source Jar uses six Archwood Slabs and two glass-block-tag ingredients. An Agronomic Sourcelink uses two Source Gems, two gold ingots and one wheat. These are individual shipped recipe totals, not a complete workshop bill of materials. [A07]

## Steps

1. **Make the chamber before building a power network.** Its pattern is `xyx / x x / xyx`, where `x` is Archwood Planks and `y` is gold ingots. Put amethyst or lapis into the chamber to obtain Source Gems according to the shipped guide. The chamber passively accumulates Source for recipes; a jar is a speedup route, not a documented prerequisite for the first gem. Exact processing time and input/output count for this conversion were not captured. [A07, A08]
2. **Build a Source Jar beside your crop area.** The recipe has slabs across the top and bottom, with glass at middle left and right. Craft an Agronomic Sourcelink with Source Gems top-center and bottom-center, gold at middle left/right and wheat in the center. [A07]
3. **Use natural growth to supply it.** The documented collection range is 15 blocks for crop and tree growth. Put the jar within five blocks of the Sourcelink for output. The guide expressly says bonemealing crops does **not** grant Source, so do not spend bone meal to diagnose an empty jar. Magical plants provide bonus Source, but ordinary crop/tree growth is already the described trigger. [A09]
4. **Move a filled jar within two blocks of the chamber for faster processing.** For recipes with extra pedestal ingredients, the guide places those pedestals within one block of the chamber and says their items are not consumed. Do not apply that non-consumption rule to the different Enchanting Apparatus. [A08]
5. **Prepare the Enchanting Apparatus as a separate station.** Its recipe uses two Sourcestone, four gold nuggets, two gold ingots and one diamond-tag gem. The Arcane Core uses six Sourcestone, two gold ingots and one Source Gem. Each Arcane Pedestal uses four Sourcestone, four gold nuggets and one Source Gem. Obtaining Sourcestone and selecting a first complete apparatus recipe remain outside this captured material chain. [A10]
6. **Load only a verified apparatus recipe.** The guide requires an Arcane Core next to the Apparatus base, ingredient pedestals within three blocks and the recipe's middle item used on the Apparatus last. Check that recipe's Source cost rather than assuming every craft is passive or free. The exact core geometry and a completed apparatus output still need an authorized runtime check. [A10]

## Expected result

The documented handoff is a Source Gem supply and naturally replenished jar feeding the chamber, followed by a prepared apparatus plan. The player-visible checks are gem conversion and jar accumulation; neither was observed by this authoring task.

## Common failures

## Tips with trade-offs

- **Wait for natural growth before spending bone meal.** Prerequisite: an Agronomic Sourcelink and nearby jar. Benefit: tests the actual documented source trigger without wasting bone meal. Trade-off: slower feedback while crops grow. [A09]

## Evidence status

Reference and intermediate how-to from hash-matched shipped book entries and recipes. Not runtime tested. Structure IDs and recipe fields are verified; exact code-defined multiblock geometry, per-device operating range and effective server settings remain unresolved.

## Prerequisites

Keep an Environmental Eye in your hotbar and retain Gold Leaf. You need the Ritual of the Forest setup and its book structure page for the following crafts. Avoid adding passive consumers near your main garden before you can measure the surroundings. Aura is environmental; a full internal device buffer does not establish a healthy surrounding area. [N04, N05]

## Steps

1. **Bottle the right Aura for the first token.** The Aura Bottling entry directs you to craft a **Bottle and Cork**, then interact with the air while holding it. Use the Overworld for the first Token of Joy: the recipe specifically requires `naturesaura:aura_bottle` carrying `aura_type = naturesaura:overworld`. A generic glass bottle or the same item with Nether Aura is not that ingredient. Craft it shapelessly from one glass bottle and one plank-tag item. This is the book's `naturesaura:bottle_two_the_rebottling` recipe; the plain bottle by itself is not sufficient. [N06]
2. **Make Tokens of Joy.** At the Forest ritual, use a central oak sapling plus six stand ingredients: the Overworld Aura bottle, one Gold Leaf, one small-flower-tag item, one apple, one torch and one iron ingot. The shipped recipe produces two `naturesaura:token_joy`. Grow the central sapling to start, as in the beginner route. [N07]
3. **Make a Natural Altar.** Use a central oak sapling, three stone, one Gold Leaf, one gold ingot and one Token of Joy in the Forest ritual. The result is `naturesaura:nature_altar`. This crafts the block; it does not complete its surrounding multiblock. [N08]
4. **Build the altar's separate structure from the book.** Open The Natural Altar entry and match structure `naturesaura:altar`. It uses Golden Stone Bricks among its construction materials; one Stone Bricks plus one Brilliant Fiber crafts one Golden Stone Bricks item, shapeless. An exact total and spatial placement cannot be given until the code-defined structure is independently reviewed. Do not assume the Forest ritual's powder pattern is also the altar's structure. [N08]
5. **Infuse one ordinary iron ingot.** Once the completed altar has collected sufficient Aura, place the ingot onto it and wait. The exact recipe is iron ingot → one Infused Iron, Aura cost **15000**, serialized time **80**. The field's time unit was not independently traced, so this is not a promise of an 80-second wait. The book describes internal collection from the surroundings and a depletion limit, not an unlimited resource. [N09]
6. **Plan replenishment rather than adding consumers indefinitely.** The Herbivorous Absorber consumes nearby small flowers to create Aura and functions only in the Overworld according to its entry. Repeating the same flower reduces its effectiveness; the guide recommends roughly six different kinds for maximum efficiency. Supply variety rather than a single flower type. [N10]

## Expected result

A measured first infusion, followed by a replenishment plan whose input costs you understand. This is not a fully closed automated system. The absorber's recipe uses four plank-tag ingredients, three Infused Iron, one peony and one Token of Joy. A peony is a construction ingredient here; the operating text calls for **small flowers**, not an assumption that every decorative flower works as fuel. [N10]

## Common failures

- **The altar block exists but will not operate:** check its separate multiblock, stored Aura and input recipe. The declared altar chapter advancement is `naturesaura:aura_bottle_overworld`; that explains book presentation, not proof of a universal block-use gate. [N08, N09]
- **The Eye reads half full:** that is the expected default environmental level. The Eye has a display ceiling and floor and cannot quantify farther beyond them. [N04]
- **A generator emits red sparks:** the book describes this as the production ceiling. Some generators stop; others keep behaving without producing more Aura. Do not blindly add more input material. An Aura Detector is the documented control lead; wiring and settings are not researched here. [N11]
- **A generator seems ineffective after relocation:** new generation first repairs a depleted nearby area, and Aura can migrate. Check the Environmental Eye over time rather than expecting a local instant spike. [N05]
- **The absorber runs poorly on the same flower:** diversify the sequence; the shipped entry explicitly describes repetition loss. In the Nether it has no effect at all. [N10]
- **Passive consumption is becoming hazardous:** stop adding consumers and retreat from the affected area. The later Aura Imbalance Ward is documented to stop an adjacent consumer when local Aura is too low, at no operating cost; its own recipe/access chain is not completed here. [N12]

## Tips with trade-offs

- **Measure before building a larger consumer array.** Prerequisite: an Environmental Eye. Benefit: gives a baseline for distinguishing normal Aura from depletion. Trade-off: the basic Eye cannot measure beyond its range. [N04, N05]
- **Rotate the absorber's flowers.** Prerequisite: the Herbivorous Absorber in the Overworld and several small flower types. Benefit: avoids the documented repetition penalty. Trade-off: a diverse supply is harder to replenish than one monoculture; no exact six-flower threshold or operating radius is asserted. [N10]
- **Treat bottling automation as an item-collection job too.** Prerequisite: Bottle and Corks plus a dispenser. Benefit: the book describes automatic Aura bottling. Trade-off: filled bottles are expelled, not pulled back into the dispenser; add a collection route such as a hopper rather than leaving valuable outputs loose. No throughput or cross-mod item transfer was tested. [N06]

## Prerequisites

Identify the exact ritual you intend to run in the Dictionary. You need its matching pentacle, activation item and ingredients, not merely a generic chalk circle. ## Steps

1. **Check the center and ingredient roles.** The golden bowl activates the ritual; ordinary sacrificial bowls hold ingredient items. Keep the ingredient bowls within eight blocks horizontally of the pentacle center. Their exact decorative positions do not matter according to the bowl entry. The first-ritual walkthrough separately describes its four bowls as within eight blocks of the central bowl; use a same-level nearby layout rather than assuming vertical freedom. [O08]
2. **Match the drawing, not its decorative glyph shapes.** The chalk instructions say color and position matter, while repeated clicks cycle a decorative sign. Stop redrawing a correctly positioned mark merely because its symbol differs from an illustration. A higher ritual may need different chalk; basic chalk is not a universal substitute. [O09]
3. **Check the activation book type and binding.** The shipped guide says the correct tier of Book of Binding must be bound using the Dictionary. 4. **Read the waiting state before adding more items.** Grey particles with no ingredient consumption mean the ritual may be waiting for a specified item use or sacrifice. Check that exact ritual's instructions; do not provide an arbitrary animal or valuable item. The golden bowl's documented redstone states are 0 inactive, 1 waiting for sacrifice, 2 waiting for item use and 8 running. These are output strengths, not seconds or progress percentages. [O11]
5. **If you are automating, distinguish activation from output collection.** A hopper or pipe can insert the activation item into the golden bowl to start the ritual. The guide warns that automation of rituals producing tamed animals/familiars instead creates untamed ones. A down-facing sacrificial bowl above the golden bowl can hold crafted item outputs instead of leaving them on the ground, up to three blocks higher; do not treat that as guaranteed entity capture. [O12]

The guide proposes naming a Dictionary in an anvil, configuring patterns with that Dictionary if needed, and using that same Dictionary in subsequent crafts so the produced names agree. This is documented advice, not a tested AE2 integration. [O13]
- **Ingredients are present but ignored:** check ordinary versus golden bowl roles and the eight-block horizontal limit before changing the ritual. [O08]
- **The drawing looks different:** position and chalk color matter, not the decorative symbol. [O09]
- **Grey particles persist:** consult the explicit sacrifice/item-use conditions; adding more ordinary ingredients does not answer a different waiting requirement. [O11]
- **A calling book does not capture something:** it needs the same supported spirit type. Do not infer boss/player capture or use a main-world entity as a probe. [O14]

## Tips with trade-offs

- **Use repeatable book names for planned automation.** Prerequisite: an anvil, Dictionary and a pattern-based crafting system. Benefit: removes one documented source of component mismatch. Trade-off: existing random-name patterns may need reconfiguration; no integration was tested here. [O13]
- **Use redstone state as a question, not a success certificate.** Prerequisite: a way to read the bowl's signal. Benefit: distinguishes waiting from running without supplying random items. Trade-off: it does not identify the missing sacrifice/item; the exact recipe still must be read. [O11]
- **Keep crafted item outputs off the floor.** Prerequisite: an appropriate sacrificial bowl mounted facing down above the center. Benefit: the documented output catcher reduces loose item handling. Trade-off: it applies to crafted items, not a general creature-containment system. [O12]
