## What this edition proves
Installed files and selected saved settings were inspected. Every active archive was indexed, but only nine archives received representative gameplay-data reading. This is not a full mechanics reference or a compatibility certificate.

[Sources and evidence](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/sources/) explain the limits. [Changes](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/changes/) records the snapshot.


## Candidate snapshot scope

This is a proposed documentation snapshot, not an applied wiki release or a tested pack release.

Four named archives were freshly hashed and their shipped metadata/text inspected: Dungeons and Taverns, Iron's Spellbooks, Tensura and Twilight Forest. Their declared mod identities and dependency tables match the retained earlier declarations; their archive hashes differ. This does not establish why they differ or that their gameplay is unchanged.

The remaining roster identities are imported from the earlier snapshot. A separate named-file hash audit matched the other 118 existing archive filenames, but did not enumerate newly added filenames. The roster counts—120 active archives, two disabled archives and 121 active declared IDs—describe this candidate roster, not a fresh complete installed-population audit.

The snapshot timestamp records the four-archive documentation observation. It is not a new observation of saved settings, effective server/world rules, tag resolution, recipe overrides or gameplay. Saved-setting descriptions remain historical evidence unless explicitly refreshed. No game launch, recipe craft, progression award, persistence or compatibility test is implied.

Only this sanitized roster is a public-baseline candidate. Private witnesses, impact ledgers, local paths, raw archive content and lifecycle receipts must remain outside the public wiki. Guides retain claim-specific evidence and unknowns; a new snapshot label alone does not validate their instructions.

## Connections we can and cannot claim
- Reliquified Twilight Forest ships recipes referencing Twilight Forest items. That is concrete cross-mod recipe data, not a successful craft or a tested progression shortcut.
- AE2 and MI are both installed. Their power/automation interface has not been established by this mapping, so do not assume their cables or energy units connect directly.
- Tensura-Leveling and SoloCraft have separate declared IDs. Similar names do not prove shared levels, skills or awakening.
- An advancement's parent can position it in the UI. It does not automatically impose a gameplay gate. Some criteria do contain explicit checks; those need to be read individually.

## Coverage
All **120 active top-level JARs** are indexed: nine have representative gameplay data read, 93 have resource-path classification only, and 18 are metadata-only/unmapped. The **121 declared top-level mod IDs** are a different count. Two JARs are disabled. Nested libraries are not additional active top-level mods.

The active archives contain 9,950 recipe JSON entries, 4,498 advancement JSON entries and 2,626 loot-table JSON entries. These are shipped files, not final runtime IDs or working recipes. Conditions, tags, overrides, generated data and server settings can change what is available.

## Customization boundary
The saved enabled resource-pack list includes an icon pack and local client-resource repair packs. Static inspection found assets rather than server data in those selected ZIPs. This does not prove a live reload or a repaired gameplay feature. Global Moonlight datapack and coremods directories were empty when inspected. Generated resource providers, final override precedence and world/server configuration remain unresolved.

# Troubleshooting

> Development snapshot: **dev-0.5-20261015-r2**. Minecraft **1.21.1**, NeoForge **21.1.250**. The pack is still changing.

## Recipe search is missing or in the wrong place
The saved EMI search sidebar is right and Center Search Bar is off. Ctrl+O toggles EMI visibility in its context. JEI also has overlapping bindings, so verify what actually responds before changing mods. A missing category or transfer is not proof that the recipe itself is broken.

JEI's optional synthetic tag-recipe browsing is saved off. Normal JEI integrations and EMI recipe import have not been exhaustively tested by this mapping. Do not describe all JEI features as disabled.

## Map and inventory controls overlap
M is saved for both Xaero's map and FTB Chunks' map. C, Z and right bracket also have contextual overlaps among installed features. Check the relevant Controls entries and the screen you are in. The overlap alone does not establish a broken binding.

## Shadows are missing
Iris is saved enabled with MakeUp Ultra Fast 9.5g selected, but the shader override explicitly saves shadow casting off. That explains the saved intent, not a live rendering result. Older notes about keeping shadows do not override the current saved value. No setting has been silently corrected by this handbook.

## An optimiser appears disabled
AE2 Crafting Optimizer has its compiled graph and authoritative planner saved off, while other optimisation domains and execution features remain on. That is not an all-off state. Saved switches do not prove compatibility, active runtime use or a speed gain.

## Claims or force-loading differ from this handbook
FTB global files contain templates and defaults. Existing worlds, teams, permissions or server configuration may override them. This edition does not establish effective quotas, protection rules or operator access. Do not rely on a claim as tested protection of valuables until the server policy is checked.

## Terrain or distant rendering behaves strangely
Distant Horizons is installed. Saved generation mode is FEATURES with SURFACE_THEN_CHUNKS, eight threads and runtime ratio 1.0. Those are observations, not a recommended fix or a hard CPU cap. Rendering distant terrain does not keep distant machines simulated.

Do not transplant a separate trial's configuration or patch into this baseline. A static inspection or menu startup is not proof that generation, boss encounters, reloads or save/reopen work correctly.

## Reporting a problem
Give the pack revision, relevant mod/system, action, expected result and what happened. Redact account data, addresses and world locations before sharing any diagnostic material. This wiki does not collect logs or private saves.
