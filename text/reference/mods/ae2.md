## What the retained reference supports
The shipped small-network outline names an ME Drive, cells, Terminal or Crafting Terminal, Energy Acceptor and ordinary cables; it does not require a controller. This is a preparation outline, not a proven powered searchable inventory.[1]

Preserve flawless budding certus: the shipped guide warns that breaking it with a pickaxe, including Silk Touch, degrades it irreversibly.[1]

## Missing tasks
- Beginner: complete component/material acquisition and a compatible power bootstrap, then demonstrate searchable deposit and retrieval.
- Advanced: channels/device expansion, autocrafting setup and recovery, and exact input/output/filter/overflow behavior.
- Integration: MI energy conversion or item/fluid interface is unknown; co-presence is not compatibility. AE2 Crafting Optimizer is a separate identity, not an AE2 progression branch.
- Runtime: successful craft, powered access, recovery and save/reopen remain unconfirmed.

Stop before supplying unverified power or transferring stock. A dark terminal does not by itself diagnose a missing controller. Use the linked starter's common-failure checks, not random additional power mods.[1]
