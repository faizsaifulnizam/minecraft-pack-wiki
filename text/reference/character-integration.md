## Three identities, not three independent courses
- **tensuraleveling** describes itself as the compatibility mod between Tensura: Reincarnated and Solo Leveling: Reawakening.
- **tldebug** describes detachable corrective safeguards co-packaged with Tensura Leveling.
- **tladdons** describes detachable UI and compatibility extensions co-packaged with Tensura Leveling.

These are direct metadata descriptions. They do not explain how a player toggles them, which changes are active, or whether you can safely remove one component. This handbook does not recommend removing or enabling a component.

## Explicit integration versus unproved synergy
The archive contains a mixin declaration naming SoloCraft mana regeneration/read paths, Tensura existence-storage mana projection/regeneration, shared-world helpers, shadow continuity and unified combat helpers. This establishes explicit integration intent beyond simple co-presence. A filename is still not an implementation proof: the mana conversion formula, writer coverage, regeneration rules, combat-slot behavior and awarding conditions remain unresolved.

The selected writer manifest identifies **SLR 1.3.1**, source commit **8579e5a282581b1da66504459455cd785594e810**, while this snapshot selects SoloCraft **1.3.3**. Do not promote that manifest to exact coverage of 1.3.3. It specifically says a later writer surface must be reviewed explicitly.

## Returning-reader diagnostic checklist
1. Identify whether the symptom belongs to [SoloCraft's panel](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/guides/solo-start/), a [Tensura helper screen](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/guides/character-tools/), or a cross-system transition.
2. Record the values actually displayed before and after the action, including which resource label changed. Do not treat Mana, Magicules, EP or a character level as interchangeable units from their names.
3. If one system changes and the other does not, report that discrepancy rather than forcing a reset, an award command or a configuration change. This lane has no validated cross-system recovery procedure.
4. Keep the two progression records separate until an exact handler and its conditions are traced. A compatibility declaration is not permission to invent a combined leveling curriculum.
