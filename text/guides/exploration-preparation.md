## Prerequisites
- A small replaceable travel kit: food, light, building blocks, a tool and protection appropriate to what you can already handle. No minimum armour tier is established here.
- Spare recovery equipment left at a known safe base; avoid taking every valuable item on the first trip.
- Permission from your group to begin a dimension visit. Arrange who returns first and who stays clear of the entrance.
- Read [Dimension travel and return](https://faizsaifulnizam.github.io/minecraft-pack-wiki/guides/dimension-travel/) before spending portal materials. It distinguishes the shipped hint from an unconfirmed construction/exit procedure.

## Steps
1. Make the first outing a reconnaissance trip, not a clear-everything expedition. Choose one objective: inspect a potential route, confirm an already-established crossing, or survey the outside of a building you encounter naturally. Do not search a saved world or copy another player's map.
2. Reserve enough food, light and blocks for the retreat as well as the outward leg. Keep the return approach clear. These are suggested supplies, not a recipe or a proven ability to bypass a barrier.
3. For dimension travel, keep valuables behind until the group has actually established that entry **and return** work under its rules. A rendered portal, an advancement toast or arrival alone does not establish round-trip safety. Do not break or redecorate the crossing while someone is away.
4. At any unexpected protection, dangerous weather or locked-biome message, retreat along the known route rather than repeatedly trying to force entry. The shipped messages say “Biome Locked!” and “Check your advancements”; they do not say better armour is the solution. Read the current advancement text from safety, or opt into the full reference below. [S1]
5. For an overworld structure, inspect the exterior and retreat route before taking loot or descending. A building's familiar style is not a guarantee of neutral occupants, safe floors or an accessible chest. The selected surface example declares a surface generation step, not a safety guarantee. [S2]
6. Return before extending the route; deposit only if the organization handoff in the conditional J1 chain has separately been established. This manuscript does not establish that handoff. If you die, re-equip at the safe base first. Do not assume grave protection, item permanence, a safe respawn in the dimension, a teleport command or that another player received your progression. None was established by this evidence. If the recovery route remains lethal, pause rather than chaining deaths.

## Expected result
A bounded objective, expendable kit, reserves for a retreat and an agreed stop condition. This page does not promise a discovered structure, a working portal or a completed progression stage.

Useful tip: when your group is ready for a new crossing, treat the return check as its own objective before combat. Benefit: less gear is exposed while the route is uncertain. Trade-off: a shorter first expedition and possibly an aborted trip.

## Common failures
- **“It is in the mod list, so it must exist nearby.”** A structure JSON is a declaration, not a world observation. Seed, biome eligibility, existing chunks, overrides and runtime generation remain unverified.
- **“The new item means the area is unlocked.”** Inventory, advancement completion and actual protection removal are separate observations; retreat if they disagree.
- **“Death will bring me home with my gear.”** Respawn destination, recovery mods and item-loss rules were not inspected. Plan as though recovery may require another dangerous trip.
- **“A character ability will bypass this.”** No cross-mod bypass or character-system integration was proved. These pages do not inherit a Twilight curriculum from the existing character notes.
